Kitchen Table design system

Material 3 roles carrying the brand palette: deep violet primary, three accents for calm, alive and care, cream kept for tonal moments. Built for a non-profit London community that browses and introduces rather than swipes.

Foundations

Colour & surface roles

The canvas is warm white and cards are pure white with a hairline and a soft shadow, so separation comes from elevation rather than a cream-on-cream wash. Cream is a deliberate tonal moment — care, boundaries, community notes — never the whole screen.
