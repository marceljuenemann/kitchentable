Our commitments

These commitments are part of our Terms of Use and are binding on us.

  • Core features stay free. Creating a profile and connecting with other members will never be behind a paywall.
  • Your data isn't for sale. We won't sell your personal data, and we won't use it, or let anyone else use it, to target advertising.
  • Built with the community. We'll develop the Community Guidelines together with members, and any member can give feedback and share their views before we make significant changes.
  • Profits are reinvested. Any profits go back into running and developing Kitchen Table. Pay for anyone working on it stays reasonable and in line with comparable roles.

Your responsibilities

Our Terms of Use set out the full rules. In particular:

  • Treat people with respect. No harassment, pressure or abuse, and no unsolicited sexual messages or images.
  • Respect consent. Clear, ongoing consent from everyone involved, on the app, on dates and at events.
  • Protect each other's privacy. Don't share anyone's identity, profile, photos or messages outside Kitchen Table without their permission.
  • No nudity or explicit content, and no photos showing anyone under 18.
  • Take responsibility for your own safety. Use your judgement, especially when meeting someone for the first time.
