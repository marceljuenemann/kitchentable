# Kitchen Table Privacy Policy

**Version 0.1 · Effective from 15/10/2026**

This Privacy Policy sets out how HTTP 403 LIMITED collects, uses, shares and protects personal data in connection with Kitchen Table, and the rights you have in relation to that data. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

## 1. Who we are

1.1 Kitchen Table is operated by HTTP 403 LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 16781742, whose registered office is at 483 Green Lanes, London, England, N13 4BS ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**").

1.2 We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy for the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("**UK GDPR**") and the Data Protection Act 2018. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office ("**ICO**") under registration number **ZC274843**.

1.3 Our director, Marcel Juenemann, is responsible for data protection matters and can be contacted at **privacy@kitchentable.dating**.

## 2. Scope and definitions

2.1 This policy applies to personal data about:

- (a) Applicants and Members;
- (b) people who enter their email address to sign in or create an account; and
- (c) people who contact us, or who are named in a report or complaint made to us.

2.2 Terms defined in our Terms of Use, including "**Kitchen Table**", the "**Service**", "**Member**", "**Applicant**" and "**Content**", have the same meaning in this policy.

2.3 "**Special category data**" has the meaning given in Article 9 UK GDPR and includes data concerning a person's sex life or sexual orientation, including data from which these can reasonably be inferred.

3.3 **Photos.** Photos are cropped and re-encoded on your device before they are uploaded. This removes embedded metadata, including any location at which the photo was taken. We do not receive or store the original file.

3.4 **Age verification.** Age verification is carried out by our provider (see section 7). The provider first estimates your age from a selfie, which does not identify you. Only where that estimate is inconclusive will the provider ask for an identity document and compare its photo with your selfie. As that comparison involves biometric data used to confirm your identity, we ask for your explicit consent on that screen before it begins. If you do not give consent, the age check cannot be completed and we are unable to approve your application. We do not receive or retain your selfie or identity document.

3.5 **Data we do not collect.** We do not collect phone numbers, precise location data, contact lists, payment details or information about criminal convictions or offences.

3.6 **Mandatory and optional data.** Your email address and the required profile fields are necessary for us to provide the Service. If you do not provide them, we are unable to process your application. Fields marked as optional may be left blank.

3.7 **New profile fields.** We may add fields to profiles as the Service develops. Information you enter in a new field is processed for the purposes, on the lawful bases and for the retention period set out above for profile data. Before collecting a type of data that this policy does not describe, or that section 3.5 states we do not collect, we will update this policy in accordance with section 16.

3.8 **Usage statistics.** Usage statistics record only how the Service is used, such as which pages and features are opened. They do not record the content you enter, which profiles you view, or with whom you connect or communicate.

## 4. Personal data we receive from others

4.1 We may receive personal data about you from other people, in particular:

- (a) the invite code in your sign-up link, which identifies the Member who invited you; and
- (b) safety reports from Members or third parties concerning your conduct.

4.2 Where a report or complaint includes personal data about another person, we use that data only to deal with the matter concerned. We do not disclose the identity of a person who makes a report to the person reported, unless the reporter agrees or we are required to do so by law.

## 5. Consent

5.1 After you confirm your email address, and before you provide any other information, we ask for your explicit consent on a separate page. Until consent is given, your account cannot hold a profile or photos and is not visible to any other user. We record the date and time of your consent and the version of the wording shown to you.

5.2 Your consent covers the use of your special category data to operate and improve Kitchen Table, including reviewing your application, displaying your profile to Members and facilitating connections with other Members.

5.3 You may withdraw your consent at any time. Because the Service cannot be provided without this data, withdrawing consent requires your account to be closed. To withdraw consent, email **privacy@kitchentable.dating** from the email address associated with your account, and we will delete your data in accordance with section 9. You may also edit or remove any profile information at any time without closing your account, or ask us to hide your profile from other Members.

5.4 Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it was withdrawn.

## 6. Who has access to your data

6.1 **Application review.** Your profile, photos, application answers and invite code are accessible only to the person reviewing your application, who is currently our director. Any other person who assists with review or moderation does so under a written confidentiality agreement.

6.2 **Members.** Once you are a Member, your profile and photos are visible to other Members. Your application answers, email address and invite code are not shown to Members.

6.3 **Public.** Profiles are not visible to anyone outside Kitchen Table.

6.4 **Disclosures required by law.** We may disclose personal data where required by law, for example in response to a valid request from the police, or where we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to prevent serious harm to any person. We disclose only what is necessary and, where permitted by law, will inform you.

## 8. Commitments on the use of your data

8.1 We do not sell or rent personal data.

8.2 We do not use personal data, or permit any third party to use it, to target or personalise advertising, and we do not share personal data with advertisers.

8.3 We do not use personal data or Content to train artificial intelligence models.

8.4 We do not use third-party analytics or tracking pixels.

## 9. Retention and deletion

9.1 We retain account data until you ask us to delete it. This applies to all accounts, including applications under review, applications on the waiting list and applications we have declined. When we decline an application, we will inform the Applicant that they may ask us to delete their data.

9.2 Following a request to delete your account, we will delete your account, profile, photos and application answers within **30 days**. Copies held in database backups are overwritten within a further 7 days.

9.3 Safety reports and moderation records are retained for 12 months after the matter is closed, regardless of whether the account concerned has been deleted, so that removed Members cannot rejoin by deleting their account.

## 10. Security

10.1 We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

- (a) encryption of data in transit and at rest;
- (b) private photo storage, accessible only through time-limited links;
- (c) server-side access controls preventing Members from directly accessing one another's data;
- (d) passwordless authentication by email link; and
- (e) restricting access to personal data to those who require it.

10.2 Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Where the risk is high, we will also notify the individuals affected without undue delay.

## 13. Cookies and local storage

13.1 We use only cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to provide the Service. We do not use cookies for advertising, analytics or tracking. The Service stores the following on your device, using cookies, your browser's local storage and its cache:

- (a) your sign-in session, so that you remain signed in;
- (b) your unfinished application, so that your answers are retained if you leave the page;
- (c) copies of content you have already loaded, such as profiles, photos and, where available, your messages, so that the Service loads faster and remains usable without an internet connection; and
- (d) security cookies set by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, to protect the Service against automated abuse and attacks, for example to record that your browser has passed a security check.

13.2 These items are strictly necessary to provide the Service you have requested, and consent is therefore not required. Usage statistics do not store any data on your device.

13.3 Data in items (a) to (c) remains on your device until you sign out, when the Service deletes it. Security cookies expire automatically, typically within 30 minutes to a day. Anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to see this data, so you should sign out after using a shared device and protect your device with a passcode.

## 14. Automated processing

14.1 We may use automated tools, including an artificial intelligence service, to assist in reviewing applications and reports, for example by summarising an application or identifying possible concerns. These tools do not take decisions. A person takes the final decision on every application and every moderation action.

14.2 We do not take decisions about you based solely on automated processing within the meaning of Article 22 UK GDPR. Before introducing any feature involving such processing, such as suggested matches, we will update this policy.

If we become aware that we hold personal data about a person under 18, we will close the account and delete the data, except where retention is necessary to protect that person or to report a concern to the relevant authorities.
