# Kitchentable — Product Doc

This doc outlines ideas for building a non-profit dating app.

## Hypothesis

- The tech behind dating apps is fairly trivial, especially in the age of AI
- A non-profit dating app might provide a much better user experience than for-profit apps that charge for basic features such as seeing likes. Could such an app spread entirely through word of mouth without a significant marketing budget?
- Can such an app reach the initial critical mass of users by targeting a narrow group like the London ENM community?

## Mission & Principles

- **Facilitate authentic & healthy connections through technology and community building**
- **Quality over quantity**, in terms of users and in terms of connections
- **Opinionated**. Informed by latest scientific evidence, have an opinion on what tends to lead to satisfying connections and relationships, without judging people. Encourage emotional intimacy and transparency in what people truly desire
- **Inclusive.** Be inclusive to marginalized groups, especially queer, ENM, people with disabilities

## Business Model

Self-funded initial development, run via the founder's Ltd. The London ENM community is small enough that maintaining an app exclusively for this community shouldn't require full-time staff, especially if community members are happy to help out with some tasks (e.g. moderation).

If the app is successful and expands beyond London's ENM community, the plan is to create a Community Interest Company (CIC) to enshrine the non-profit model legally. At that point some revenue would be needed to professionalize and guarantee continued development.

Potential funding ideas:

- **Grants.** Many grants are available for CICs, and positioning the app as tackling the loneliness epidemic seems promising
- **Donations.** Asking users for donations at the right moment, e.g. after they've set up a date. Or possibly a "supporter" badge for subscribers without offering any special features?
- **Partnerships.** Promoting trusted relationship coaches and therapists (or their courses) inside the app
- **Events.** Running in-person dating events or relationship skill courses

Preference is to steer well clear of any user payments or freemium models, as they tend to compromise the user experience. If needed, there might be transparent and ethical ways to do it (e.g. a limited number of boosts, clearly marked as such).

**Governance:** The goal is an active community around the app, but not a democratic decision-making model — trying to make everyone happy tends to produce compromised products that nobody really loves. Better to have a leadership team of 2-3 people with diverse perspectives who do a good job listening to the community.

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

> The goal is to validate the hypothesis above as quickly as possible. So instead of coming up with fancy new features from the start, just build a basic dating app along the lines of existing apps and see if it can reach critical mass.

### Making Connections

- Require an intro message instead of just liking a profile
- Select what type of connections you are interested in (ENM dating, friends etc.)
- Limit to 3 (?) intros / likes per day for people to be more selective and spend more time on quality introductions
  - Therefore, swiping is probably not the right UX. Probably just show people a list of all people matching their criteria (like OKCupid used to do) with the ability to dismiss people.
  - If we're that selective, potentially an option to "save" / bookmark profiles might be required — although that might encourage people to optimize too much?
